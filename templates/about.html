Our Mission

To democratize precision medicine by making advanced pharmacogenomic dosing accessible, safe, and efficient for every clinician worldwide.

Who We Are

Bridging the Gap Between
Genomics and Clinical Care.

GenMedix started as a research initiative to solve a critical problem: Adverse Drug Events (ADEs) caused by improper Warfarin dosing. Traditional "trial-and-error" methods put patients at risk of bleeding or clotting.

By integrating Machine Learning with Pharmacogenomics (PGx), we created a system that predicts the optimal dose instantly, factoring in genetic markers like CYP2C9 and VKORC1 alongside clinical data.

Precision

Moving beyond "one size fits all" to "one dose per patient."

Safety

Real-time checks for drug interactions and contraindications.

Innovation

Continuous learning algorithms that improve with new data.
